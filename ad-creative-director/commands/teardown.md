Read `_memory/` for context, then tear the ad down using the `competitor-teardown` skill.

If `_memory/` doesn't exist, tell them to run `/onboard` first and stop.

The ad to tear down: $ARGUMENTS

Handle these before you start:

- **Nothing given** — check `Swipe/` for anything you haven't already logged a teardown for in
  `_memory/Log/`. If `Swipe/` is empty, ask them to save a screenshot, transcript, or video there.
- **Only a brand name or a link** — say you can't tear down an ad you can't see. Ask for the
  transcript, a screenshot, or the file. Never reconstruct an ad from a company's reputation.
- **No run-time information** — say the read is structural only. You have no performance data for
  anyone else's ads and you never estimate it.
- **They want you to copy it** — take the argument, not the sentences. Rewrite in their voice from
  `Audience.md` and say that's what you did.

Every teardown ends in at least one concept they could actually run this week, sized to the gear
and talent in `Preferences.md`. A teardown with no output is a book report.

Write it to `_memory/Log/Teardown-<competitor>-YYYY-MM-DD.md`. In chat: the angle, the one thing
worth stealing, the one thing to skip, and the concept it suggests.
